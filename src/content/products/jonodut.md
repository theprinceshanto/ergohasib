## What it does

- **One platform, many leaders.** Each leader gets their own site and workspace.
- **Bangla and English.** Content is drafted in both languages side by side.
- **Draft, review, publish.** Changes are staged and checked before they go live.
- **Verified identity badges** show visitors they are on a leader's real site.
- **Constituent management** keeps the people a leader serves in one place.
