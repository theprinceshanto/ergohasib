## What it does

- **Daily notes** are seeded from a planner of recurring tasks, timed reminders and habits.
- **Cards for insights, ideas and references** link to each other. Insights and ideas start raw and become linked once they connect to something.
- **Areas and projects** group notes through each note's own `area` or `project` property.
- **Today view** shows reminders, tasks, habit streaks, the next seven days, active projects and raw cards.

## Your notes stay yours

Everything is plain Markdown with YAML properties, so notes stay readable and editable even with the plugin turned off.
